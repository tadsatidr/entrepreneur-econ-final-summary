No-calculator habits that cover all three blocks

Read the sign before the size. Own-price slopes are negative. Cross-price positive means substitutes; negative means complements. Income positive means normal; negative means inferior.
Elastic versus inelastic is a comparison with 1. If the quantity percent change is visibly larger than the price percent change, it is elastic. You rarely need the third decimal.
Stars are three comparisons. p below 0.01 gets ***. p below 0.05 but not 0.01 gets **. p below 0.10 only gets *. p above 0.10 gets no star.
An insignificant slope has elasticity 0. Do not multiply it by a ratio of means. The Chapter 2 table sets advertising and GPP elasticities to 0 for this reason.

Each section page has a fuller no-calculator box next to the formulas those questions use. The formula sheet collects them on one page.
