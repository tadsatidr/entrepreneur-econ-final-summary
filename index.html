No-calculator habits that cover all three blocks

Sign before size.Own-price slope negative. Cross-price + substitutes, − complements. Income + normal, − inferior.
Compare with 1, not to three decimals.If quantity’s % change is visibly bigger than price’s, demand is elastic.
Stars = three comparisons.p < 0.01 ***; < 0.05 **; < 0.10 *; above 0.10 none (insignificant → elasticity 0, “no effect”).
Largest coefficient = most important (within one regression on the same scale): attitude 0.317.
Percent of a round number.25%, 50%, 80% of 10,000 → 2,500, 5,000, 8,000. Split awkward products: 2,450 × 72 = 2,450 × 70 + 2,450 × 2.
Averages of two weights.(70 + 30)/2 = 50; (30 + 0)/2 = 15. Outside the cheap–expensive zone → 0.
